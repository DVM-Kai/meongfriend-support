The service tracks the user's requested walk route, time and distance. Delaying or interrupting it would lose route points. Android device diagnostics confirmed a foreground location service while the screen was off. The user can end the walk using the visible End Walk button; the test walk was ended after capture. Optional sharing follows the user's location visibility and family sharing choices. This stationary demonstration is not a walking-distance accuracy test.
