Privacy policy

Effective October 10, 2026. Operator and privacy contact: DVM-kai / Son Gyu-yeol, umyfriendss@gmail.com. This policy covers the current TripRibbon closed-test features for adults aged 18 or older. Reading this policy or registering does not enable location recording or sharing.

Account and trip data

Email, display name and authentication details support registration, authentication and account restoration. Passwords are handled by the authentication service and are not shown to travelers. Server-linked itineraries, traveler names, polls, comments, notices, spending, packing lists and pet preparation data are shared with trip participants. Device-only trips are not automatically uploaded to the account server.

Optional information

Contact details are stored only when you register them for notifications. Automatic email and SMS delivery is not connected at present. Precise location is collected only when you start recording and sharing; only selected travelers receive it. Server records are cleaned up after at most 24 hours. You may decline permissions or stop recording. Device notifications support preparation reminders.

Reports and blocks

Report targets, content copies, reasons, notes, reporter accounts and timestamps are stored for operator review. Reporter account details are not shared with travelers. Reports are periodically deleted after 90 days from submission. Blocks are kept with the server trip. Consent version and acceptance time are deleted with the account.

Retention and deletion methods

Account, authentication and consent data support your account while it is in use; server trip data is retained while that trip is kept. App deletion APIs remove data, subject to the shared-record exceptions below. Contact support for retry if deletion fails. Expired request-limiting records are periodically cleaned up. Reports are retained for 90 days from submission plus the next hourly cleanup cycle.

Processors and international handling

Supabase Pte. Ltd. processes account, trip, authentication and request/security-log data for authentication, database/server operation and authentication-email delivery. The primary database is in Seoul, South Korea; support, email and subprocessor facilities are not limited to Korea and may include Singapore, the United States and other supplier locations. Feature requests use encrypted network transmission. Supplier data is retained during the service contract or until supported deletion requests are processed; logs and internal copies follow supplier contract and plan policies. Account deletion does not guarantee instant removal of every supplier copy. References: https://supabase.com/legal/dpa and https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/logs. No separate operator-managed automatic database backup is configured for this free project. The Meongfriend Pro seven-day backup terms do not apply to TripRibbon.

Maps, sharing and support

Opening maps, search or sharing links may send your selected location, query or shared content and connection details to Google Maps, a search service or your selected email/messaging app under its policies. Support email includes the address, message and attachments you choose to send, retained as needed to handle the inquiry and rights request. Support pages use GitHub Pages, where GitHub may process IP addresses and technical access data. This app currently has no advertising, payment or behavioral analytics features.

Deletion and rights

Delete account in My account to delete server trips you own and revoke traveler access. For trips owned by others, your access, contacts and location sharing are removed; shared names and expenses may remain. Other travelers’ device copies may remain. Delete local copies separately from the trip menu. Contact support to request access, correction or removal of shared records. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Rights, safeguards and changes

Contact support to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or an end to international processing. We use minimal information such as your registered email to verify identity and explain the outcome and any necessary steps. Refusing required authentication/server processing limits account features; device-only planning remains available. Safeguards include encrypted transmission, membership checks, protected device authentication storage and separate server secrets. Shared trip content is not end-to-end encrypted. Material processing changes will be announced in the app and public pages; new consent is requested where needed.
