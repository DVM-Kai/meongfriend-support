Delete in the app

In TripRibbon, open My account, choose Delete account, confirm your current password and the deletion scope, then confirm deletion. Use Forgot password from Sign in if needed.

Without app access

Email umyfriendss@gmail.com with the subject ‘TripRibbon account deletion request’. Include your registered email and whether you request full account deletion or specific trip data removal. The operator will explain identity verification and scope. Never send passwords, verification codes or original identity documents.

Deletion scope

Server trips you own, traveler access to those trips, your account and consent records are deleted. In trips owned by others, your access, contacts and location sharing are revoked; shared names, expenses and others’ device copies may remain. Report evidence is kept for 90 days from submission; its reporter-account link is removed on account deletion. Delete device copies separately. The current free project has no separately configured operator-managed automatic database backup. Provider security/access logs and system copies may remain for periods governed by the provider agreement, plan and legal obligations; immediate deletion of every copy is not guaranteed. Email requests are processed after identity and scope verification, with the result sent to the requester. See the data notice for provider processing, international transfers and retention.
